Watchword Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

The short version: Watchword has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your words and reflections stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. The developer does not collect, see or sell any of your data.

What Watchword stores

Your weekly words and reflections are stored on your iPhone. They're shared between the Watchword app and its widget on the same device so the widget can show your word.

Your settings (dial color, typeface, soundscape, breathing preferences) are stored only on your iPhone.

iCloud backup

If you're signed in to iCloud and iCloud is on for Watchword, your weekly words and reflections are also kept in your own iCloud account, so they survive a new phone or a reinstall. This uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it. To turn it off, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, and switch off Watchword.

Search

Past words and reflections can appear in Spotlight on your iPhone so you can find a week quickly. This index stays on your device. To hide Watchword from search, go to iPhone Settings → Apps → Watchword → Siri & Search.

Apple Intelligence

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, short definitions and the "For you" word suggestions are generated on your iPhone using Apple's on-device model. Your words and reflections are not sent anywhere for this.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Siri, Shortcuts or the Breathe control with Watchword, your request is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Watchword only receives the action itself, such as the word you set or the note you add.

Online definitions (off unless you turn it on)

Watchword can look up a short definition for a word that isn't on its built-in list. This is off unless you turn it on in Watchword's Settings → Privacy → Look up definitions online. When it's on, and Apple Intelligence isn't available, only the word itself is sent to the free Datamuse service (datamuse.com), which draws on Wiktionary and WordNet. Nothing that identifies you is included, though, like any internet request, the service may see your device's IP address. Definitions are saved on your iPhone so each word is looked up only once.

Purchases

Watchword Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells Watchword only whether Plus is unlocked. The developer never sees your name, payment details or Apple Account.

Notifications

The Sunday reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. It is not sent from a server.

Children

Watchword does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
