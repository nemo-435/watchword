Common questions

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Watchword. To change its background or soften its color, touch and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget. Tapping the widget opens a breathing session.

How does the breathing work?

Watchword uses the 4-7-8 pattern: breathe in for 4, hold for 7, breathe out for 8. The iPhone's Taptic Engine pulses with each phase, so you can close your eyes and follow along. You can turn the pulses off and choose the number of cycles in Settings.

When does the week change?

Weeks run Sunday to Saturday. On Sunday evening Watchword can remind you to wind in your next word. You can change the reminder in Settings.

I bought Watchword Plus. How do I get it on a new phone?

Open Watchword → Settings → Watchword Plus → Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account. Plus also works for family members through Family Sharing.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

Will I lose my past words if I change phones?

Not if iCloud is on for Watchword. Your words and reflections are kept in your own iCloud account and come back when you sign in on a new iPhone.

Does Watchword collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. Read the privacy policy.
