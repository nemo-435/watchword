A word for the week.

Choose one word on Sunday. Carry it through the week on a calm, watch-dial home screen, and breathe with it whenever you need to.

For iPhone

How it works

Wind the week

Each Sunday, look back in one line, then hold to wind in your next word. Stuck? Watchword offers a few to start with.

Breathe

Tap the widget for a 4-7-8 breath guided by gentle Taptic pulses, so you can follow it with your eyes closed. Deep soundscapes play underneath.

Look back

Every week lands on a year dial, a quiet journal of what you've been holding onto.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your words stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Read the privacy policy.
